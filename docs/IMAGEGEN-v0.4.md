# Materiales generados para v0.4

Generados con la herramienta integrada `image_gen` el 5 de septiembre de 2026. Los originales permanecen en la carpeta de imágenes generadas; las copias usadas por Unity están dentro del proyecto. Los atlas se muestrean por cuadrantes mediante shaders, sin recortar o modificar los PNG generados.

## ground

Archivo: `RiskAI/Assets/RiskAI/Resources/Painted/GroundAtlas.png`.

Prompt exacto:

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production albedo texture atlas for a 3D fantasy real-time strategy game. Generate one square 2048x2048 image divided EXACTLY into four equally sized square material tiles in a 2 by 2 grid, no gutters or borders. Top left: lush mid-tone emerald and olive grass made from small angular painted blade tufts, subtle irregular clumps, no flowers. Top right: warm ochre packed earth with little pebbles and painted brush strokes, very few cracks, no large rocks. Bottom left: worn irregular light warm gray courtyard flagstones, chunky shapes and dark crevices with tiny moss tufts. Bottom right: dark forest leaf litter with olive clover and small leaves, moss and brown soil. Style: richly hand-painted fantasy game textures reminiscent of Warcraft III environment art, clear brushwork, strong local detail, restrained contrast readable from an elevated RTS camera, modern resolution. Flat orthographic material scans, no perspective, no objects, no 3D render, no scene, no shadows from external objects, no labels or text or watermarks. Each quadrant individually seamless and evenly lit, fills its quadrant edge to edge. Material detail is small enough to repeat over a battlefield. The four quadrant boundaries must be straight at exact midlines. These will be used on real mesh terrain, not as a backdrop illustration.

## architecture

Archivo: `RiskAI/Assets/RiskAI/Resources/Painted/ArchitectureAtlas.png`.

Prompt exacto:

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production albedo texture atlas for a 3D fantasy real-time strategy game. Generate one square 2048x2048 image divided EXACTLY into four equal square tiles in a 2 by 2 grid without gutters, frames or labels. Top left: hand-painted medieval limestone masonry wall, large warm pale gray rectangular stones with chiseled beveled edges, dark mortar, subtly ochre worn surfaces; scale about 5 blocks wide and 7 courses high. Top right: rich cobalt blue slate roof shingles, about 9 staggered rows, slightly curved chunky edges, painterly worn blue edge highlights and deep navy overlap shadows. Bottom left: dark warm brown medieval oak boards, pronounced irregular wood grain, 7 vertical boards, golden brown worn edges, no metal nails. Bottom right: dense mass of rich green broadleaf tree canopy, overlapping small pointed leaf clusters with forest green shadows and olive/lime highlights, no gaps or sky; painterly fantasy foliage. Style: richly hand-painted fantasy RTS Warcraft III-like stylization with strong bevels and readable material shapes, modern resolution. Flat orthographic material sheets, evenly lit, not photorealistic, no scene, no perspective, no 3D cubes, no text, logos or watermark. Each quadrant individually tileable, fills its area fully, exact straight central boundaries. These are textures to be mapped on original modeled buildings and trees, not concept art.
